1. 适用范围

本隐私政策适用于「入学必备720字」（英文名 HanziRoots，Android 包名 com.ailexin.HanziRoots，以下简称「本应用」）。本应用是一款面向学龄前与低年级儿童、海外华裔少儿及中文初学者的离线汉字学习工具。

2. 我们收集的信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。本应用没有注册、登录、账号系统，不会要求或记录姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、设备标识符（IMEI / Android ID / 广告 ID）等信息。

本应用不申请网络访问权限（INTERNET），所有学习内容、插画与语音均随安装包内置，运行过程中不会向任何服务器发送数据。

3. 仅保存在您设备本地的数据

为了提供「断点续学」和「收藏复习」功能，本应用会在您的设备本地保存以下学习状态：

  • 各阶段、课程、部首、笔画分组的最近学习位置；
  • 星标收藏的汉字列表；
  • 界面语言选择、英文释义开关、引导页是否已阅读等偏好设置。

上述数据通过 Android 系统提供的本地存储机制保存，仅存在于您的设备中，不会上传、同步或备份到我们或任何第三方。卸载本应用即会删除这些数据，您也可以在系统「应用信息 → 存储」中一键清除。

4. 权限与系统能力说明

  • 网络权限：未申请。应用在飞行模式下功能完整。
  • 语音朗读（TTS）：应用内置全部点读音频，优先离线播放。仅当内置音频缺失时，才会调用设备自带的系统语音引擎（该引擎由您的设备厂商或系统提供，其数据处理受其自身隐私政策约束，我们无法访问其内容或日志）。
  • 存储：仅使用系统分配给本应用的私有目录，不读取您的照片、文档或其他应用的数据。

5. 第三方 SDK 与广告

本应用不包含广告 SDK、数据分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK、社交登录 SDK 或任何用户追踪组件。所使用的开源组件（如音频播放、本地偏好存储）均只在设备本地运行，不联网、不回传数据。

6. 数据共享与出售

由于我们不收集任何数据，因此不存在向第三方共享、转让、公开披露或出售数据的情形。我们也不会基于用户数据进行任何形式的画像或定向推荐。

7. 儿童隐私（COPPA / GDPR-K）

本应用面向儿童设计。我们严格遵守儿童隐私保护的相关要求：

  • 不收集任何儿童（或成人）的个人信息；

由于不存在服务器端数据，本应用不涉及数据传输与远程存储的安全风险。本地学习数据保存在应用私有目录，受 Android 系统沙箱保护，保留至您卸载应用或清除数据为止。

In one sentence: This app runs fully offline. It does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, and contains no ads, in-app purchases, accounts, or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to “入学必备720字” (English name: HanziRoots; Android package com.ailexin.HanziRoots, the “App”). The App is an offline Chinese character learning tool for preschoolers, early-grade children, overseas Chinese-speaking children, and beginner learners of Chinese.

2. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The App has no registration, login, or account system, and never requests or records your name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers (IMEI / Android ID / advertising ID).

The App does not request the network permission (INTERNET). All lessons, illustrations, and audio are bundled with the installation, and no data is sent to any server at runtime.

3. Data Stored Only on Your Device

To support “resume where you left off” and “starred review”, the App saves the following learning state locally on your device:

  • Your last position in each stage, lesson, radical group, and stroke-count group;
  • Your list of starred characters;
  • Preferences such as interface language, the English-gloss toggle, and whether the onboarding guide has been read.

This data is stored using the local storage provided by Android and remains on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced, or backed up to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes it, and you can also clear it via Settings → Apps → Storage.

4. Permissions and System Capabilities

  • Network: Not requested. The App is fully functional in airplane mode.
  • Text-to-speech (TTS): All read-aloud audio is bundled and played offline first. Only when a bundled clip is missing does the App call the device’s built-in system speech engine (provided by your device manufacturer or OS, and governed by its own privacy policy). We cannot access its content or logs.
  • Storage: The App uses only its private app directory and does not read your photos, documents, or other apps’ data.

5. Third-Party SDKs and Ads

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login, or user-tracking SDKs. The open-source components used (e.g., audio playback and local preference storage) operate entirely on-device and do not connect to the network or report data.

6. Sharing and Selling Data

Because we collect no data, we do not share, transfer, disclose, or sell data to any third party, and we do not build user profiles or run behavioral targeting of any kind.

7. Children’s Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

  • We collect no personal information from children or adults;
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, and no user-generated content;
