```
HanziRoots is an offline Chinese character app for preschoolers, early-grade children, overseas Chinese families, and anyone starting Chinese from zero. It distills 720 high-frequency characters into one card that teaches shape, sound, meaning, and writing together. No internet needed — open it and start learning.

■ 720 characters, a clear path
· 6 stages × 30 themed lessons × 4 characters each, ordered by difficulty
· The next stage unlocks as you finish the current one; or study everything unlocked in one run
· Three ways to find a character: by theme, by 174 radicals, or by stroke count (6 bands)

■ One card, five learning layers
· Illustration: 720 high-resolution, text-free scenes that anchor meaning
· Pinyin: standard four-line grid with clear tone marks
· Large kaiti character in a tian-zi grid, using a subset of LXGW WenKai
· Words and sentences: 3 common words and 1 contextual sentence per character, all proofread
· English gloss: one tap to show English for the character, words, and sentence

■ Stroke animation + finger tracing
· Every character is drawn stroke by stroke and can be replayed anytime
· Trace directly in the grid with a start dot, dashed direction, and end arrow
· Real-time checks on start point, stroke direction, and path fit — you advance only when it is right
· A dedicated gesture area so writing a horizontal stroke never flips the page

■ 3,380 offline audio clips
· 720 characters + 1,954 unique words + 706 sentences, all bundled in the app
· Tap the character, a word, the sentence, or the picture to hear it; the picture plays sentence → word → character
· No Chinese voice pack and no network required — works on tablets, TV boxes, and projectors

■ Five interface languages
· Simplified Chinese, English, 日本語, 한국어, Tiếng Việt — switch anytime
· First launch follows your system language, and stroke names are localized too

■ Parent-friendly by design
· Runs fully offline; the app does not request network access
· No ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, no accounts
· Collects no personal data; progress is stored only on your device

Made for: ages 3–8 learning their first characters, heritage families abroad, absolute beginners, and classroom or family co-reading.

Note: the download is large because every illustration and audio clip is built in, so nothing else needs to be downloaded later.
```
